# Telemedicine Eligibility

Video consultations are available for General Medicine, Dermatology, Pediatrics and follow-up visits in other specialties. Telemedicine is not suitable for conditions that need a physical examination, such as new fractures, chest pain or severe breathing difficulty. A first consultation for Orthopedics or Cardiology must be in person, but later follow-ups may be done by video. Patients need a stable internet connection and a valid government photo ID to start a video consultation.
