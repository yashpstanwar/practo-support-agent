# Appointment Booking Policy

Patients can book an in-clinic or video appointment through the Practo app or website up to 30 days in advance. Each booking is confirmed instantly by SMS and email with a unique appointment ID in the format APT-XXXX. A patient may hold at most two upcoming bookings with the same doctor at any time. Walk-in patients are seen only if a slot is free, and booked patients always get priority.
