# Consultation Fee Structure by Specialty

Consultation fees are set by specialty and by the doctor's seniority. General Medicine consultations cost between 300 and 600 rupees, and Pediatrics consultations cost between 400 and 800 rupees. Dermatology consultations cost between 500 and 1000 rupees, and Orthopedics consultations cost between 600 and 1200 rupees. Cardiology consultations are the most expensive, ranging from 800 to 1500 rupees, and every fee includes GST.
