# Follow-up Visit Discount Policy

A follow-up visit with the same doctor within 7 days of the original consultation is free of charge. A follow-up booked between 8 and 14 days after the original consultation receives a 50 percent discount on the consultation fee. After 14 days, a follow-up is charged as a regular new consultation. The discount applies only when the doctor has marked the original appointment as needing a follow-up.
