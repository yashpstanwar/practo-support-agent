# Cancellation and Rescheduling Window

Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 4 hours before the scheduled slot. Cancellations made less than 4 hours before the slot forfeit 50 percent of the prepaid consultation fee. Each appointment can be rescheduled a maximum of two times, after which it must be cancelled and booked again. A patient who does not arrive within 15 minutes of the slot is marked as a No-Show and the prepaid fee is not refunded.
