# Home Visit Eligibility

Home visits are available for patients above 65 years of age, bedridden patients and patients recovering from surgery within city limits. Home visits can be booked for General Medicine, Pediatrics and Orthopedics, as well as for home sample collection for lab tests. A home visit carries an additional travel charge of 300 rupees on top of the regular consultation fee. Home visits must be booked at least 12 hours in advance and are available between 8 am and 8 pm.
