# Lab Test Turnaround Times

Routine blood tests such as a complete blood count or lipid profile are reported within 24 hours of sample collection. Specialised tests such as thyroid panels and vitamin levels take 48 to 72 hours. Culture tests and histopathology reports can take up to 7 days. Reports are uploaded to the patient's Practo health records, and the patient receives an SMS as soon as a report is ready.
