# Insurance Claim Process

Patients can claim reimbursement for consultations and lab tests if their health insurance policy covers outpatient care. To start a claim, upload the itemised invoice, the doctor's prescription and your insurance policy number in the Claims section of the app within 30 days of the visit. Practo forwards the documents to the insurer, and most insurers settle or reject a claim within 10 working days. Practo does not decide claim approval, and the final decision always rests with the insurance company.
